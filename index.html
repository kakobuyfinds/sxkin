From listing to QC

  1. Find the original listing. Follow the source link from the CAFANS product page when available. If you use HooBuy, paste the original seller URL into the agent’s product search and verify that the item resolves.
  2. Record the exact variant. Save the link, color, size and seller’s size chart. For oversized tops, compare chest width and body length with a garment you own. For footwear, check whether the chart shows foot length or insole length.
  3. Review warehouse photos. If your order includes QC photos, check the size tag and color against your order. Request a measurement photo when fit depends on a specific dimension, and check exchange deadlines before releasing the parcel.
  4. Compare shipping quotes. Use the same destination, package dimensions and weight for each quote. Check whether the line bills by actual or volumetric weight.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product shortlist for comparing source links, sizes and prices before ordering through a shopping agent. This independent guide uses CAFANS product listings as its starting point.

Why does the price change when I open a listing?

Variants, seller updates and exchange rates can change the displayed amount. Check the price for your exact size and color, then review shipping and fees in your agent’s quote.

What if the agent cannot open the product link?

Open the original seller listing in your browser and confirm it still loads. Copy the seller URL from that page. If the agent still cannot resolve it, check whether it supports the marketplace or offers manual ordering.
